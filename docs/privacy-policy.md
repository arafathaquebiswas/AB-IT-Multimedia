# Privacy Policy — AB IT Multimedia

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

This privacy policy describes how the Android app **AB IT Multimedia**, developed by **AB IT**,
handles information. The same policy is shown inside the app on the About screen.

## Summary

AB IT Multimedia does **not** collect, store or share any personal data. It has no user accounts,
no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

## Information used only on your device

- **Message for the custom broadcast.** The text you type on the Broadcast Receiver screen is passed
  only between the app's own screens, using an Android broadcast that is restricted to the app
  itself. It never leaves your device and is not saved once you leave that screen.
- **Battery level and charging status.** While the battery screen is open, the app reads Android's
  system battery broadcast only to display these values. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Video and music.** The sample video and music are stored inside the app.

## Internet use

The app connects to the internet only when you open **Image Scale**, to download one photo from
[Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos) (picsum.photos), a free third-party image service, over an
encrypted HTTPS connection. As with any website, Lorem Picsum's servers receive the basic technical
information that every internet request contains, such as your device's IP address. The app sends
no other information, and AB IT does not receive or store any of it.

## Permissions

- **Internet access** (`android.permission.INTERNET`) — to download the Image Scale photo.

The app requests no other permissions. (It also declares an internal permission that only the app
itself can hold; Android uses it to keep the custom broadcast private.)

## Storage, retention and deletion

A copy of the downloaded photo is kept in the app's private cache so it does not need to be
downloaded again. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or
by uninstalling the app. AB IT keeps no data on any server, so there is nothing to request or delete
from us.

## Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The app holds no personal data, the custom broadcast can only be
received by the app itself, and none of the app's receivers can be reached by other apps.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
